About Suzhou Vigor New Materials

Suzhou Vigor New Materials Co., Ltd. is a professional manufacturer specializing in the research, development, production, and sales of high-performance acoustic panels. Headquartered in Changshu City, Jiangsu Province — one of China's most dynamic manufacturing hubs — we have been serving architects, interior designers, contractors, and distributors worldwide for over 15 years.

Our mission is to create acoustic environments that enhance human experiences — whether in a corporate boardroom, a luxury hotel lobby, a recording studio, or a peaceful home theater. We believe that great sound and great design are not mutually exclusive; they should work together in perfect harmony.

With a state-of-the-art manufacturing facility, advanced CNC production lines, and a dedicated R&D team, we offer five distinct product series: Wood Veneer Acoustic Panels, PS Salt Crystal Acoustic Panels, Hexagon Wood Acoustic Panels, Curved Flexible Acoustic Panels, and Special Shape Wood Acoustic Panels. Each series is engineered for specific aesthetic and acoustic requirements, and all products can be fully customized to meet your project needs.

We export to over 50 countries and have completed more than 500 successful projects worldwide. Our commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction has made us a trusted partner for leading architects, designers, and construction companies across the globe.
